How to read this

An expectation is a written statement of what a metric should do over a set of dates, with a range. It is recorded in a registry that can only be added to. When the data for its dates is loaded, the pipeline computes the observed value and gives a verdict.

The expected ranges here come from an AI agent acting as a subject matter expert, interviewed and checked by the engineer. The full interview is public. In real use the experts would be people.

Where the gap comes from
For expectations about a change between two periods, the gap between observed and expected is split by slices such as service or entry point. Every slice is assumed to have been expected to move by the same percentage. This shows where a gap sits. It does not say why.
